What we build · engage for any or all

Four builds we deliver most often. Every one working somewhere real.

No feature lists. Each build is scoped like a job: clear duties, a printed limit, and a reference you can check. What the industry calls AI agents, we treat as employees: hired for one job, held to its limits, supervised by a human.

Role No. 01

The Receptionist

Answers your line on the first ring. Every call, all night, all weekend. Takes messages, answers the questions you approve, and sends a summary you can read in ten seconds.

One limit, printed on purpose: when it isn't sure, it says so and takes a message for a human. No guessing. Ever.

Reference: answers the front line of an immigration law office in North Carolina. Live for months. Invoiced. Paid.

Role No. 02

The File Clerk

Reads everything you have. Twenty years of quotes, contracts, emails, job photos. Then answers in plain words and shows you the exact page the answer came from.

One limit: if it isn't in your files, it says it isn't there.

Reference: working inside an e‑commerce company's stack today, reading years of tickets and records so every answer arrives with the page it came from.

Role No. 03

The Bridge

Carries data between the two systems that refuse to talk, so nobody retypes numbers from one screen into another. Daily. For years.

One limit: a human approves anything it writes. If it can't verify a match, it queues the record for a person instead of forcing it.

Reference: matches court emails to a law office's case files automatically. A person reviews every match before it lands.

Role No. 04

The Web Front Desk

A site like this one. A film of your business, rendered on our own machines, with an estimator that gives honest ballparks and a desk that books the visit.

One limit: the estimates it quotes are ranges you set. It never invents a price.

Reference: we built a painting contractor his whole new site, with a working estimator, before he agreed to anything. You're reading another one right now.

Role No. 05 · open

The Open Requisition

The four above are what we build most often — not the only things we build. Dispatch, scheduling, estimating, the books — describe the shift that eats your evenings and we'll design the job, build the agent, and fit it to the systems you already run.

One limit: every custom hire gets one, written down before it clocks in. If we can't print the limit, we don't build the job.

Reference: every position on this page started here — as a job nobody had built yet.

Where your files work

Your data stays yours.

Machines in North Carolina that we physically own and run. For most jobs your files go there. Not to a big AI company's cloud. Nothing you give us ever trains anyone's model.

When a job genuinely needs a cloud model, we say so first. You decide. Where your data lives is a design decision we make with you, not fine print.

Why we built the trust gate

In an early test, our own AI confidently invented a caller's email address. It sounded completely sure. It was completely wrong.

So we rebuilt the system so that can't happen: what you said is the record, and the AI is never allowed to “correct” it. When it isn't sure, it flags a human instead of guessing.

Every receptionist we ship carries that gate. It's why a law office trusts one with its front line.
