Someone copied your store.

Same logo, same photos, same product, on a domain you don't own, running ads on your name. It takes your customers' money and ships nothing. Then those customers call you. We find these stores, prove what they are, and get them taken down.

How you'll notice

The signs

  • Customers call or email about orders you have no record of.
  • Your brand shows up in ads you didn't buy.
  • A domain like yourbrand.shop, yourbrand.us, yourbrand-official.com or us-yourbrand.com appears.
  • Your photos, your logo, even your phone number, on a site that isn't yours.

That last one is the cruel part. The fake store often lists your real number, so the people it robbed call your staff, angry about orders you never took.

Why it matters

What it costs you

  • Your support team absorbs it. Every robbed customer is a call, an email or a bad review with your name on it.
  • They come back. Clone crews register spare domains in advance. One report rarely ends it.
  • Nothing alerts you. No one broke into your systems. They copied your public website, so none of your security tools sees a thing.
  • Enterprise tools are priced for enterprises. The big brand-protection platforms are built and priced for big companies. Smaller brands are exactly who these crews go after.

Getting started

What we need from you

  • Your real domains and stores, so we never flag you.
  • Your trademark registration, if you have one.
  • A short authorization letter, so we can file as your agent where platforms allow it.
Straight talk

This is not legal advice. Sworn trademark notices are signed by the trademark owner, and we will tell you which ones those are. Some platforms act within hours, some take weeks. We keep the pressure on and tell you where every report stands.
